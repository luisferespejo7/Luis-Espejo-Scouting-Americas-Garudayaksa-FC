Scout Context
The Americas — spanning Brazil, Argentina, Colombia, Mexico, USA, Ecuador, Uruguay and beyond — represent an underexplored and underpriced talent pool for Asian and Oceanian leagues. Compared to European imports, Americas players offer strong physical profiles, adaptability, and competitive pricing. Liga 1 Indonesia (2025/26) now allows 11 foreign players per squad with 8 on the field simultaneously, creating a structural opportunity to sign Americas-based talent at Liga 1 level as a pipeline strategy.

Age Recommendation for Garudayaksa FC
Target Window: 21–26 years old. Players aged 17–20 are ideal long-term investments but require development runway and adaptation time. Players over 28 bring experience but limited re-sale value. For Liga 1 Indonesia, the sweet spot is 23–26 with first-team experience in a South American first or second division. Physical peak aligns with Liga 1 intensity demands, and adaptation curve is manageable within half a season.

Core Scouting Philosophy
Sign Them Before the Market Does
The window is before these players explode. Target players at Segunda División / B Nacional / Expansión level who are actively scanning for a first division opportunity — Liga 1 Indonesia is exactly that door for them. They will accept Indonesia salary ranges precisely because they have not been discovered yet. Sign them young, develop them at Liga 1 intensity, and capture the value before a European or bigger Asian club notices.

Age sweet spot: 19–23. Enough experience to contribute immediately. Ceiling still untouched.

⚠ Priority Action
Indonesia Field Trip
Before any player proposal is finalized, a visit to Indonesia is essential. Remote scouting and data analysis can only go so far. To recruit accurately for Garudayaksa FC, the scout must experience the Liga 1 environment firsthand — understanding the pace of play, physical demands, climate conditions, tactical tendencies of opponents, referee standards, and pitch quality.

This trip serves two purposes: (1) assess the real challenges a foreign Americas player will face on arrival — adaptation speed, language barriers in the dressing room, lifestyle adjustment — and (2) identify the specific gaps in the squad that Americas recruitment can realistically fill. Any player profile built without this context risks being built on assumptions, not evidence.

Phase 2 — Analysis
🔬
Data Analysis
  • →Break season into 8-game blocks — assess consistency, not peaks
  • →Build ranking per position per league every 8 weeks
  • →Key metrics: xG, Club Score, Goals Added, Passes Packed, % Crossing, 1v1 Duels
  • →Add physical layer: Max Speed, HSR, HI Count, Accelerations
  • →Data is always a bias — context is everything
🖥️
Platform Benchmark
  • →Big Data: Impect, StatsBomb, Opta, Driblab, FBref, ASA
  • →Physical/Game IQ: Skill Corner (most advanced — runs, pressure, passing execution)
  • →Regional: GolStats (MX), Eyeball (Africa), Delphlyx
  • →Transfer Tools: TransferRoom, Libro de Pases, Recruitment Hub (Pelly)
  • →Similarity tools: "Who plays like Player X?" — most used predictive element
📋
Player Report
  • →Platform templates (Wyscout, Driblab) or own club format
  • →Voice notes or short questionnaires first — full report for AAA priority only
  • →Tournament reports: cover events, assess level, spot future targets
  • →AI support: Peak AI most interesting tool emerging in the market
  • →Always cross-reference: data + video + live or trusted reference

📝

There is no winning formula. Cross-Reference Principle
Every player assessment must combine at least two independent sources: data platform (FBref / Sofascore / Wyscout) + video analysis, ideally supplemented by a live observation or trusted reference. A strong data profile that does not hold up on video is not a recommendation. A strong video impression without data support requires further investigation before proceeding. Consistency over 8-week windows is more valuable than single-game peaks.
